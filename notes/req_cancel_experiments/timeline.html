The relay keeps its own connection to the backend alive after the client's side dies — it just drops what it can't deliver. Uvicorn never sees a disconnect.

Takeaway: cancellation today relies on the transport propagating a TCP disconnect — true over a direct socket and through Kong, false through the VS Code tunnel. To make it reliable everywhere, add an explicit cancel signal (beforeunload → /chat/cancel) instead of depending on however the network in between behaves.
